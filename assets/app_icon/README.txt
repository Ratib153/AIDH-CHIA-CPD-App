Place your launcher icon source image here as:

  icon.png

Requirements:
- Square (1:1), ideally 1024 x 1024 pixels
- PNG format
- Simple design that reads well at small sizes (phone home screen)
- Prefer transparent background, or a solid CHIA blue (#0082C8) background

Then from the project root run:

  flutter pub get
  dart run flutter_launcher_icons

Rebuild and reinstall the app on your device to see the new icon.
